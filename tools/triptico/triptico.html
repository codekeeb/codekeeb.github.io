Getting started

Each half has its own controller and battery. The left half talks to your computer; the right half connects to the left one on its own.

  1. ChargeCharge both halves over USB-C, one at a time.
  2. Switch onSlide the power switch on each half to on.
  3. WakePress any key.
  4. Pick a profileHold RAISE+1. This selects Bluetooth profile 1.
  5. PairOn your computer, open the Bluetooth settings and connect the new keyboard that shows up.
  6. LightsThey start off. Turn them on with LOWER+R.

Wired

Plug the left half into your computer and it also types through the cable.

Battery and sleep

After a minute idle the lights go off; after 30 minutes it goes into deep sleep. Any key wakes it up. The lights are what use the most power.

Keys to know

Hold LOWER or RAISE and the other keys change function; let go and they come back.

RAISE+1–5Bluetooth profile 1 to 5 RAISE+EscClear the active profile LOWER+RLights on or off LOWER+TNext light mode LOWER+ZUnlock for Keymap Studio

Fit the encoders

  1. Switch both halves off and unplug them.
  2. Pull the keycap under the display with the puller, then the switch below it. Keep both.
  3. Fit the encoder in its place and push the knob onto the shaft.
LeftTurn: volume · Press: mute RightTurn: scroll · Press: play / pause
